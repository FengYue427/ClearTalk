ClearTalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Summary: ClearTalk helps you draft communication messages. It is not legal or medical advice. Drafts and history are stored locally by default; if you sign in, use cloud AI, or submit feedback, some data is sent to our servers over HTTPS (see §1.3).

1. Information we collect

1.1 Local-only data

Stored in your browser (localStorage), not uploaded:

  • Generation history (scene, form values, output text)
  • Feedback on results (to improve the product)

1.2 Data sent for AI generation

  • Selected scene type
  • Form fields you fill in
  • Tone / style preference

Sensitive fields (e.g. ID numbers, phone numbers) are redacted before being sent to AI providers.

1.3 Account & server storage (when signed in)

  • Account ID and email (login and password reset)
  • Optional synced custom scenes, history, and settings
  • Feedback you submit
  • Analytics events (e.g. scene opened); IP stored as a short hash prefix only
  • Daily AI generation counts (free / Pro quota)

See also Privacy, Terms, and Disclaimer.

2. What we do not collect

  • Real-name identity documents as a dedicated category
  • Precise geolocation
  • Contacts, photos, or similar device data
  • Advertising cookies or cross-site trackers

3. Storage & security

3.1 Local-first

  • Clearing browser data removes local history
  • History does not sync across devices unless you use account sync
  • We cannot recover data stored only on your device

3.2 Your practices

  • Avoid real names and government IDs in forms when possible
  • Use roles or initials (e.g. “Manager Wang”)
  • Delete history you no longer need

4. Third-party services

AI generation may use providers such as DeepSeek over HTTPS. Their policies apply to processing on their side.

5. Your rights

  • Access — view history in the app
  • Delete — clear history or individual entries
  • Export — export localStorage via browser tools
  • Offline — view history offline when PWA is installed

6. Minors

Intended for adults. Users under 14 should use the service with a guardian and avoid sensitive personal data.

7. Updates

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted in the app and the date above will change.

Contact

  • Email: [your contact email]

By using ClearTalk you agree to this Privacy Policy.
